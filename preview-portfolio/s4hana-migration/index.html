The problem

Most of the migration risk is in what is already there.

Custom code nobody fully remembers

Years of custom programs, user exits and reports sit in the current system, often written by people who have moved on.

Rules hidden in the system

Pricing, approval and posting rules live in configuration and code rather than in a document someone can read.

Gaps found too late

Teams discover what the standard S/4HANA setup will not cover during testing, when changes cost the most.

How it works

Five steps, with your team approving each one.

  1. 01

    Inventory

    We catalogue the custom programs, tables, interfaces and reports in your current SAP system, with how often each is used.

  2. 02

    Explain

    Each item gets a plain-language summary of what it does and which business rule it carries, for your team to confirm.

  3. 03

    Compare

    We flag where the standard S/4HANA setup is likely to cover the need, and where it is likely not to.

  4. 04

    Draft

    We draft a change list: keep, replace with standard, rewrite, or retire, with the reasoning for each.

  5. 05

    Review

    Your architects and process owners review and approve every item. Nothing changes in your system without sign-off.

What you get

Documents your team can act on.

  • A searchable inventory of custom objects with plain-language descriptions
  • A gap list against the standard S/4HANA setup, with open questions marked
  • A draft change list your team can edit, approve and hand to the delivery partner
  • A short summary for management: scope, open decisions and risks
