Ledger

One desk for disbursements, repayments, and the exceptions a credit team actually handles. This page shows how Nexora would frame a brief. It is not a client engagement, and it does not report a result.

Discipline

Situation

A regional lender is outgrowing spreadsheets. Officers need one place to release a loan, see a repayment, and catch the case that does not fit the rule.

We would design the officer’s desk beside the ledger, not after it. Every figure on screen would point at the entry behind it. Access and audit would be part of the same piece of work, with a named owner.

What we would make

  • An officer desk for the day’s cases
  • A ledger with an audit trail a person can read
  • Roles for branch, credit, and finance
  • An access log for every change
