What it does

  • Matching across sources

    Reads a bank statement (CSV or MT940), MTN MoMo and Airtel Money exports and the general ledger, then matches records by reference and amount, by amount, date and payer, or one payment against several invoices. Each match shows its reason.

  • Anomaly flags

    Rule-based flags for duplicates, round-number spikes, missing counterparties, amounts far from history, off-hours payments and reversals, each with a plain reason.

  • Daily summary draft

    Writes a draft daily summary: match rate, position per source, inferred fees, items that need attention, open items and suggested next steps.

  • Human review before anything is posted

    Everything is marked as a draft for review. The demo does not post to the ledger, move money or send anything; a person decides what happens next.
